AI-powered cauliflower health checks, quality grading, and market insights to support smarter crop decisions for farmers.

AICW — Artificial Intelligence and Career for Women
Program & Industry Partners

Introduction

CauliVision helps farmers check cauliflower health using a photo captured with a phone or uploaded from a device. It estimates potential disease risk and produce quality, helping farmers spot problems early and make more informed crop and harvest decisions.

The project also provides estimated market prices and practical crop-care guidance, including advice for black-soil cultivation. These results can help farmers prepare produce for sale and plan next steps, while predictions remain estimates that should be checked with local agricultural experts when needed.
